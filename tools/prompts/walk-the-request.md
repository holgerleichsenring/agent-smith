Follow one request through this system, end to end, and say where each part of it lives.

Pick the group of entry points where the most authority is concentrated, and say why you
picked it. Then, for that group, answer these six questions with a file and a line you
actually opened:

1. How is the request admitted?
2. What does the caller present as proof of who they are?
3. How does that proof become an identity this system knows?
4. What is that identity permitted to do?
5. Which objects may it reach?
6. What does the operation change or produce?

Whatever construct answers a question here is the answer — a decorator, a filter, a base
class, a hand-written call. If something genuinely has no home here, say so: "authorization
is role-only, nothing scopes by object" is a complete answer and often the important one.

Then say what looks wrong to you, and why. If you are unsure, say it anyway with your
doubt attached — missing something matters more than being wrong.
